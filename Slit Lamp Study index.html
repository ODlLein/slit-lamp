    Set-up and order

    Set-up

    • Disinfect chin and forehead rest; wash hands.
    • Lateral canthus at the canthus marker.
    • Focus eyepieces, set the PD.
    • Start low mag, low light, diffuse.
    • Light from the temporal side.

    Order: outside → in

    Lids & lashes→Conjunctiva→Tear film→Cornea→AC + Van Herick→Iris→Lens→Ant. vitreous→Fluorescein last

    Van Herick grades

    Narrowest beam, 60° temporal, microscope straight ahead, beam perpendicular at the limbus. Compare the dark gap with corneal thickness (CT). Try it in the simulator above.

    GradeGap : CTAngleAction
    4≥ 1Wide openSafe to dilate
    31/4 – 1/2Incapable of closureUsually safe
    21/4Narrow, closure possibleGonioscopy before dilating
    1< 1/4Very narrowDon't dilate; refer
    00ClosedRefer

    Exam pearls

    • Microcysts = reversed illumination; vacuoles = unreversed.
    • Cells/flare need a dark room (Tyndall effect).
    • Flare 3+ = iris details hazy.
    • Always add the yellow filter with fluorescein.
    • Examine the iris before dilation (rubeosis).
    • Cortical & PSC → retro from the fundus, dilated.
    • Record location, size, depth, drawing and grade.
    • Van Herick is screening; gonioscopy is the gold standard.
